Patata Privacy Policy

Effective date: [effective date]

This privacy policy explains how the game Patata (the "app") for iPhone, iPad and Android handles information. Patata is made by [developer name], an individual developer based in Saudi Arabia ("we", "us").

The short version: Patata does not collect any personal data. It has no ads, no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no tracking and no third-party analytics. We do not sell or share any data, because we do not have any.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information from anyone, including children. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, birthday, photos, contacts or location.

2. Information saved only on your device

To make the game work, Patata saves a few small game settings only on your device:

  • sound and music settings and the chosen control style;
  • game progress, such as your win streak, best results and which tips you have seen;
  • whether Patata Pro is unlocked on this device.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app. Your phone or tablet may include app data in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup or Android backup), depending on your device settings; those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google, and we cannot access them.

3. Optional Tilt control (device motion)

If you choose the optional Tilt control, the app reads your device's motion sensors (how the device is tilted) to move the potato. This is processed instantly on the device. It is never stored and never sent anywhere. If you do not choose Tilt, the app does not use motion data.

4. Purchases

Patata is free to play. The optional one-time purchase Patata Pro is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, email or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from the store, on your device, that Pro is unlocked. A grown-up check (parental gate) is shown before the purchase screen. Please see the Apple or Google privacy policy for how they handle payment information.

5. Crash and performance reports

If you have allowed it in your device settings, Apple or Google may share anonymous crash and performance reports with app developers. These reports do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

6. Children's privacy

Patata is designed to be safe for children and families. Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). This is in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR rules for children, and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). The game has no ads, no links to websites, and no way to talk to other people. Purchases are behind a parental gate.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove all game data, delete the app from your device. If you have any question or request, please contact us (see below).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change is released. If we ever planned to collect any data, we would update this policy and the store privacy labels first and, where the law requires, ask for consent (including a parent's consent for children).
